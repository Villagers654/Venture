## Dev identity (Unknown Source Edition)

This fork adds an opt-in dev identity: a local developer user with no Meta
account and no ownership check, for development builds and software distributed
outside the Meta store. It applies only to apps you turn it on for. Use it only
with software you wrote, have the rights to test, or are licensed to run. It is
not intended for, and must not be used for, running store titles you have not
purchased, or for circumventing licence or entitlement checks. The maintainers
and contributors accept no responsibility for misuse, and nothing here is legal
advice.
