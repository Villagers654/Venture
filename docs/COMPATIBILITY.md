## What “Quest exclusive” means here

**Yes** means the title was available only on Meta's platforms (Quest, and Rift on PC), though it may also have versions on non-PC consoles. **No** means it also received an official PC VR release for another headset platform. This is separate from whether Venture runs the game's Quest build, which it always does.

## Imported games

Games added from the **Import** page (from a headset, an APK, a ZIP or a folder) run the same way as games installed from your Meta library. A different build of a listed game may not behave like the build tested here.

## Reporting another game

Use **Upload debug report** in Venture's Settings, or **Report issue** on the banner after a crash.

It collects the launcher and game logs from your last launch into one debug report, then uploads it as an unlisted paste and gives you a link. Attach that link when opening a [compatibility report](https://github.com/Villagers654/Venture/issues).
